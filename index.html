❮ The Club ❯

Kellett Kodes is an after-school ECA open and accessible to anyone from Y7-Y13. This term the focus is game development, with sessions on Ren’Py for beginners and Godot for more advanced coders, two amazing game engines. You’ll create your own games, by yourself or in groups (get your friends to join!), gaining first hand experience with game dev.
